# Offers & Selection Rubric

## Lead magnets (what you give away)

| # | Lead Magnet | What you give away | Why it gets attention |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | GTM System Audit | Audit their prospecting, enrichment, scoring, CRM and outbound workflow | Shows you can diagnose before selling |
| 2 | ICP & Signal Map | Identify their ideal customers + 5-10 buying signals they should monitor | Positions you around strategy, not tools |
| 3 | 100-Account TAM Sample | Build a sample list of 100 ICP accounts with enrichment + signals | Lets them see your work before hiring you |
| 4 | Outbound Leak Report | Analyze where their outbound funnel is losing opportunities | Creates a natural reason to hire you |
| 5 | GTM Automation Blueprint | Map their current manual process and show what should be automated | Demonstrates systems thinking |
| 6 | Competitor GTM Teardown | Analyze how 3-5 competitors acquire customers and where they differ | Gives executives actionable intelligence |
| 7 | Buying Signal Playbook | List of signals indicating when prospects are likely to need their product | Directly connects data to timing |
| 8 | Clay Table Teardown | Review an existing Clay table: wasted credits, weak enrichment, missing logic | Excellent for Clay-heavy companies |
| 9 | 30-Minute GTM Build | Build one small workflow live during a call | Proves technical ability immediately |
| 10 | Revenue Workflow Scorecard | Score GTM infrastructure across data, automation, outbound, CRM and AI | Creates a measurable "before" state |

## Framings (the benefit the message leads with)

| # | Framing | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | Making money | Strongest. Use when you can credibly tie the offer to pipeline/revenue ("spend $10, get $100"). |
| 12 | Saving money or time | Only when the saving is substantial (e.g. wasted Clay credits, an SDR doing hours of manual research). Never for marginal productivity gains. |
| 13 | A new mechanism | Something genuinely unfamiliar to them (e.g. job-change or hiring signals as triggers, which they clearly aren't using). |
| 14 | An audit | Show a concrete, specific problem you already spotted and what to do about it. |
| 15 | Hyper-research / case study | You understand their exact situation, or have a comparable result in `context/profile.md` (the sample profile maps recruiting, funding/investment and Clay-scoring prospects to a matching result). |

Each message combines one lead magnet (1-10) with one framing (11-15).

## Rubric: signals → offer

| Signal observed | Best lead magnet(s) | Best framing |
|---|---|---|
| Hiring SDRs/BDRs/AEs, or visibly running outbound | 3 TAM Sample, 4 Outbound Leak Report | 11 Making money |
| Clay in their stack, job posts, or team posts | 8 Clay Table Teardown | 12 Saving money (credits) or 14 Audit |
| Recently funded / scaling sales team | 2 ICP & Signal Map, 3 TAM Sample | 11 Making money |
| Product that's bought at a trigger moment (funding, hiring, expansion, compliance, leadership change) | 7 Buying Signal Playbook, 2 ICP & Signal Map | 13 New mechanism |
| Small team, founder-led sales, lots of manual work | 5 Automation Blueprint, 9 30-Min Build | 12 Saving time (only if big) |
| Crowded category, exec/founder contact | 6 Competitor GTM Teardown | 15 Hyper-research |
| Mature or messy stack (many tools, RevOps hiring, CRM migration) | 1 GTM System Audit, 10 Scorecard | 14 Audit |
| Hiring a GTM engineer / RevOps role | 5 Automation Blueprint, 1 GTM System Audit | 12 Saving time/money (the cost of the hire) |
| Industry matches a past client (recruiting, investment/funding, B2B SaaS) | whichever fits, plus the case study | 15 Case study |
| Technical founder / skeptical of agencies | 9 30-Min Build, 3 TAM Sample | 15 Proof of work |
| Agency or GTM services company (sells outbound to others) | 3 TAM Sample for one of *their* clients' ICPs, 8 Clay Teardown | 11 Making money |

### Tie-breakers
1. Choose the offer tied to the most specific, recent signal.
2. Prefer offers that show work before asking for a meeting (3, 2, 7, 6) over offers that need their access (1, 8, 10).
3. When unsure, 2 ICP & Signal Map + 3 TAM Sample is a safe default. It is the "Joe" template.
